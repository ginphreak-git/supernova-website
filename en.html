FAQ

Good to know

Does the app include channels or movies?

No. Super Nova IPTV is a player: it does not provide, sell or suggest any content. It only plays the playlist or account you enter, provided by your own service.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets (Android 8.0+), Android TV, Google TV and TV boxes, Fire TV with Vega OS, Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen (2017 and later) and Raspberry Pi.

Which Fire TV devices are supported?

Fire TV devices with the new Vega OS, such as Fire TV Stick 4K Select. The Fire TV version is coming to the Amazon Appstore.

How do I get the Raspberry Pi version?

Email us at ginphreak@proton.me and we'll reply with the options and details. You can get the image to write to a microSD or a ready-to-use microSD, even with your Wi-Fi network and your source already configured.

What do I need for the Raspberry Pi version?

A Raspberry Pi 3 B+ with its power supply, a microSD card and an HDMI cable. To control it, use your TV remote (if it supports HDMI-CEC), a small infrared receiver, a keyboard or an air mouse.

Does it work with very large lists?

Yes. It has been tested with 50,000-item playlists: the list opens in just over a second and browsing stays smooth, because the app only loads what is on screen.

Which formats are supported?

M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts. For video: HLS (m3u8), MPEG-TS, MP4 and any other format supported by your device.

My subscription allows only one connection: is that OK?

Yes. The app opens a single video stream at a time, closes it when you leave the player or switch apps, and never hammers the server if it refuses.

Which languages are available?

English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. The language is picked automatically from your device and can be changed in the settings.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your source, favorites and watch progress stay on your device. No ads, no analytics.
