FAQ

Good to know

Does the app include channels or movies?

No. Super Nova IPTV is a player: it does not provide, sell or suggest any content. It only plays the playlist or account you enter, provided by your service.

Does it work with very large lists?

Yes. It has been tested with 50,000-item playlists: the list opens in just over a second and navigation stays smooth, because the app only loads what is on screen.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes. The interface adapts to the screen: in landscape it shows the side menu and, in Live TV, the group list with channels beside it.

Which formats are supported?

M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts. Video: HLS (m3u8), MPEG-TS, MP4 and other formats supported by the device.

My subscription allows a single connection: is that OK?

Yes. The app opens one stream at a time, closes it when you leave the player or switch apps, and does not hammer the server if it refuses.

Which languages is it available in?

English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. The language follows your device automatically and can be changed in the settings.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your source, favorites and watch positions stay on your device. No ads, no analytics.
