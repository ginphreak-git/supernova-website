FAQ

Good to know

Does the app include channels or movies?

No. Super Nova IPTV is a player: it does not provide, sell or suggest any content. It only plays the playlist or account you enter, provided by your service.

Which formats are supported?

M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts. Video: HLS (m3u8), MPEG-TS, MP4 and other formats supported by the device.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your source, favorites and watch positions stay on your device. No ads, no analytics. Details in the privacy policy.

My subscription allows a single connection: is that OK?

Yes. The app opens one stream at a time, closes it when you leave the player or switch apps, and does not hammer the server if it refuses. Just avoid using the same account on two devices at once.

A channel keeps freezing: what can I do?

In the settings, raise the live channel buffer (High or Maximum) and, for Xtream accounts, try the MPEG-TS format.
